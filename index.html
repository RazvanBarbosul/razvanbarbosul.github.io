About the game

Critter Shuffle drops you into a sunny forest full of adorable animal friends — swap and match foxes, owls, ducks, deer, rabbits and toads to clear each level. Along the way you'll untangle vines, crack ice blocks, and smash crates while the critters cheer you on. It's a relaxed, feel-good puzzle game built for short sessions or long play. Every level brings new layouts, goals, and a little more forest to explore.

  • 🧩 Match-3 puzzles
  • 🦊 Cute critters
  • ⚡ Boosters & power-ups
  • 🎁 Daily rewards
